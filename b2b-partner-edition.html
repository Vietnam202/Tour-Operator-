Route & program overview

Da Nang→Hoi An→Ba Na Hills→Hanoi→Ha Long Bay→Phu Quoc

A seven-day Vietnam itinerary connecting Central Vietnam heritage, a Ha Long Bay day cruise, Hanoi cultural stops and Phu Quoc island experiences. All services remain subject to final reconfirmation.

Detailed itinerary

DAY 01

Da Nang arrival · Son Tra · Marble Mountains · Hoi An

Da Nang → Hoi An → Da Nang · Meals: D · Overnight: Da Nang

Upon arrival at Da Nang International Airport, our representative warmly welcomes you and transfers you to the hotel for check-in and refreshment. Note : Time check in 2-3 pm The journey begins with a gentle introduction to the beauty of Central Vietnam. You travel to Son Tra Peninsula, often called “Monkey Mountain,” a lush nature reserve rising dramatically above the sea. The highlight is a visit to Linh Ung Pagoda, home to the iconic 67-meter Lady Buddha Statue, guarding the coastline and overlooking Da Nang Bay. The sea breeze, the scent of pine forest, and the temple bells create a serene start to the trip. Next, continue to the legendary Marble Mountain (Ngu Hanh Son)—a cluster of five limestone peaks representing the five natural elements. You explore: Mysterious caves lit gently through stone crevices Ancient pagodas carved into the mountains Panoramic viewpoints overlooking Non Nuoc Beach In the late afternoon, the journey heads to Hoi An Ancient Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site glowing with golden lanterns and timeless charm. Follow your guide through: The 400-year-old Japanese Covered Bridge Ancient merchant houses The lively lantern market along Hoai River Dinner is served at a charming local restaurant featuring Hoi An specialties. After dinner, enjoy the magical evening atmosphere of the old town before returning to Da Nang. Overnight in Da Nang.

DAY 02

Ba Na Hills · Golden Bridge · flight to Hanoi

Da Nang → Ba Na Hills → Hanoi · Meals: B / L · Overnight: Hanoi

After breakfast and Check out room . your journey continues to the magical mountaintop world of Ba Na Hills, located 1,487 meters above sea level and famous for its year-round cool climate. The adventure begins with boarding the Ba Na Hills Cable Car, one of the longest and highest cable car systems on Earth. As the cabin ascends, you glide through layers of mist, above ancient forests, waterfalls and deep valleys. The panorama reveals spectacular views of the Truong Son mountain range stretching endlessly under the sky. Golden Bridge – Cầu Vàng Your first stop is the world-renowned Golden Bridge, a 150-meter-long pedestrian walkway appearing as if it's suspended in the clouds. The bridge is lifted by two colossal moss-covered stone hands, designed to represent the “hands of the Mountain God” pulling a golden silk ribbon from the hillside. Clients love this spot because: It offers 360-degree cloud and mountain views It produces iconic photos that became viral worldwide The early morning light creates a dreamlike atmosphere Le Jardin d’Amour – The Gardens of Love Continue your walk through Le Jardin d'Amour, a collection of themed flower gardens: The Mystery Garden with stone walls inspired by European maze sculptures The Romance Garden blooming with roses The Sacred Garden with ancient Buddhist statues The Vineyard Terrace overlooking the French Village The air here is crisp and filled with the sweet scent of flowers. Every corner is photo-worthy. Debay Ancient Wine Cellar Next, discover the Debay Wine Cellar, built by the French in 1923. It is carved deep into the mountain to maintain a natural temperature of 16–20°C year-round. Guests can: Learn about French colonial wine-making See century-old oak barrels Optional tasting of premium imported wines Fantasy Park – Vietnam’s Largest Indoor Theme Park Before lunch, you can explore Fantasy Park, inspired by Jules Verne’s novels. Inside are: Adventure rides 4D, 5D cinemas Alpine slides A massive indoor playground suitable for all ages Lunch at Ba Na Hills Lunch is served at a luxury buffet restaurant offering over 100 dishes, including Vietnamese, Asian, and European cuisine—perfect for recharging after a morning full of exploration. French Village – A Walk Through Old Europe In the afternoon, step into the enchanting French Village, designed like a European medieval town with: Gothic-style churches Clock towers Stone-paved streets Street performances such as dancers, musicians, and mimes The architecture recreates an old French town with remarkable detail—arched windows, vintage lanterns, and ivy-covered walls. It feels like stepping into a European postcard, but on a peak above the clouds. Enjoy the cool mountain atmosphere as you wander through cafés, souvenir shops, and photo corners surrounded by cloud layers drifting past the rooftops. At around 16:00, board the cable car and descend back to Da Nang, where the warm sea breeze welcomes you again. Overnight in Hanoi

DAY 03

Ha Long Bay day cruise · Sung Sot · Titop · kayaking

Hanoi → Ha Long Bay → Hanoi · Meals: B / L · Overnight: Hanoi

Sail through one of the World’s Natural Wonders – Halong Bay After breakfast, depart from your hotel for a journey to Halong Bay, one of the most breathtaking natural landscapes on Earth. A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the New Seven Natural Wonders of the World, Halong Bay features over 1,600 limestone islands and islets rising majestically from emerald-green waters. Each island is shaped by time and legend, creating a mystical seascape like no other in Asia. Your drive to Halong takes approximately 2.5 hours, passing through Vietnam’s rural countryside dotted with rice paddies, small villages, and traditional markets. Along the way, your guide shares fascinating stories about Vietnamese life and local culture. Late Morning – Begin Your Cruise Adventure Upon arrival at Tuan Chau Harbor, you’ll board your luxury day cruise. Enjoy a refreshing welcome drink and a warm greeting from the friendly crew as the vessel sets sail into the heart of the bay. As you glide through the jade waters, admire the countless limestone cliffs rising like dragon’s teeth from the sea — each formation with its own mythical name, such as: Dinh Huong (Incense Burner) Islet Ga Choi (Fighting Cock) Islet Cho Da (Stone Dog) Islet 🌿 Experience Highlight: Feel the cool sea breeze as you stand on the open deck, surrounded by majestic peaks reflecting on the emerald surface — a moment that captures the soul of Vietnam. 🍽️ Lunch Onboard – Taste of the Sea While the boat gently sails between limestone islands, enjoy a delicious Vietnamese lunch served onboard. Feast on fresh seafood delicacies such as: Steamed prawns Grilled squid Fried fish with passionfruit sauce Stir-fried vegetables and jasmine rice Vegetarian and non-seafood options are always available upon request. 🚣‍♀️ Afternoon – Kayaking or Bamboo Boat Ride For those seeking a closer connection with nature, enjoy an unforgettable kayaking experience or a bamboo boat ride around the limestone formations. Paddle through calm waters and explore hidden lagoons surrounded by towering cliffs — a chance to experience Halong’s tranquil side. Listen to the gentle rhythm of the water and the echo of birds calling from the cliffs — it’s pure serenity. 🌟 Experience Highlight: Kayaking in Halong Bay is not just an activity — it’s a way to feel the heartbeat of nature, surrounded by silence, wind, and sea. 🌄 Midday – Explore Sung Sot (Amazing) Cave Your first stop is Sung Sot Cave, known as the Cave of Surprises. Discovered by French explorers in 1901, this is the largest and most beautiful grotto in Halong Bay, measuring over 10,000 square meters. Inside, the cave opens into massive chambers filled with spectacular stalactites and stalagmites illuminated by soft, multicolored lights. Each rock formation has a story — some shaped like animals, others resembling people or mythical creatures. Your guide will share local legends and point out formations such as “The Sleeping Dragon” and “The King on His Throne.” 💎 Experience Highlight: Walking through Sung Sot Cave feels like stepping into a natural cathedral carved by time and tide — a masterpiece of nature’s artistry. 🏖️ – Titop Island Adventure After exploring the cave, continue cruising toward Titop Island, one of the most famous destinations in Halong Bay. Named after Russian astronaut Gherman Titov, who visited the island in 1962, it offers both relaxation and adventure. You may: Swim in the crystal-clear water at Titop Beach. Hike up to the island’s viewpoint (400 steps) for a panoramic 360° view of Halong Bay — a perfect spot for photos. 📸 Photo Tip: From the summit of Titop Island, the view of the bay, dotted with emerald islands and white-sailed boats, is simply breathtaking. 🌇 Evening – Return to Hanoi As the afternoon sun paints the bay in shades of gold and amber, your cruise slowly returns to the harbor. Disembark and meet your driver for the relaxing journey back to Hanoi. You’ll arrive at your hotel in the early evening, with free time to rest or enjoy dinner at your leisure. 🌅 Memorable Moment: Watching Halong’s limestone peaks fade into the mist as the sun sets behind them is a memory that will linger long after your journey ends. 🛏 Overnight in Hanoi

DAY 04

Hanoi city highlights · flight to Phu Quoc

Hanoi → Phu Quoc · Meals: B · Overnight: Phu Quoc

In morning have breakfast and check out room 🕛 8:15 PM – Pick up from your hotel Your guide and driver from Vietnam Travel Advisor will warmly welcome you at your hotel lobby. Begin your journey through the heart of Hanoi – The City of Thousand Years of Culture, a place where ancient traditions blend harmoniously with modern life. 🚉 Visit Train Street For a touch of excitement, revisit Hanoi Train Street in the evening. Enjoy a drink as the train passes just inches away from local homes — a thrilling and unique memory that captures Hanoi’s urban magic. 🏯 – Explore the Temple of Literature (Văn Miếu – Quốc Tử Giám) Continue to one of Hanoi’s most treasured landmarks – the Temple of Literature, Vietnam’s first national university, established in 1070. Dedicated to Confucius and great scholars, the temple is a masterpiece of ancient Vietnamese architecture. Walk through five serene courtyards, shaded by ancient trees and decorated with stone steles carved with the names of scholars who passed royal exams centuries ago. Feel the peaceful, scholarly atmosphere that once nurtured Vietnam’s greatest minds. 🌿 Experience Highlight: The temple’s timeless beauty and historical significance make it a favorite spot for both travelers and students seeking wisdom and good fortune. 🏛️ 2:45 PM – Visit Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Complex (Outside View) Next, you’ll visit the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Complex, an iconic symbol of Vietnam’s history and national pride. Admire the Mausoleum (outside) – a monumental structure where the preserved body of President Ho Chi Minh rests. Walk around Ba Dinh Square, where the Declaration of Independence was read in 1945. Stroll through the peaceful Presidential Palace gardens and see Ho Chi Minh’s humble stilt house, where he lived and worked from 1958 to 1969. 🕊 Experience Highlight: This sacred area offers a deep insight into the simplicity, humility, and legacy of “Uncle Ho,” Vietnam’s most beloved leader. 🌸– Tran Quoc Pagoda – The Oldest Pagoda in Hanoi Continue your journey to Tran Quoc Pagoda, the oldest Buddhist temple in Hanoi, dating back to the 6th century. Located on a small island in West Lake, the pagoda is surrounded by calm waters and ancient Bodhi trees brought from India. Admire the striking red tower reflected in the lake and learn about Vietnam’s Buddhist traditions. Capture beautiful photos of this sacred site, where serenity and spirituality meet. 📸 Photo Spot: The view of Tran Quoc Pagoda at sunset, reflected on the lake, is one of Hanoi’s most breathtaking scenes. Visit Dong Xuan Market is bigest local market in Hanoi for shopping In Afternoon our driver tranfer to airport flight to Phu Quoc Overnight in Phu Quoc

DAY 05

Phu Quoc Safari · VinWonders · Grand World

Phu Quoc · Meals: B / L · Overnight: Phu Quoc

Morning: 8h30 am start from Hotel : Visit Vinpearl Safari Phu Quoc – Vietnam’s First Semi-Wild Zoo Breakfast at your resort. After breakfast, your private car will pick you up for a fun-filled day exploring Vinpearl Safari – a must-visit attraction on Phu Quoc Island, especially for families. Location: Bai Dai Area, Ganh Dau Commune (about 30 minutes drive from most main beaches). Opening Hours: 9:00 AM Highlights of Vinpearl Safari: Open Zoo Area: Stroll through lush paths and observe over 150 species of animals from Asia, Africa, and Europe. You’ll see Bengal tigers, white lions, giraffes, zebras, lemurs, flamingos, and more. Safari Adventure (Drive-Through): Board a special safari bus and experience the thrill of being "caged" while the animals roam free – lions, rhinos, antelope, and bears surround you, just like in African safaris. Animal Performance Shows: Fun and educational shows take place twice daily, where you can see birds, reptiles, and small mammals showcasing their natural talents. Kid-Friendly Activities: Feeding giraffes, mini petting zoo, and animal photo zones are perfect for younger travelers. You’ll spend about 2–3 hours enjoying the Safari Park. Midday: Lunch suggestion (on your own): There are several restaurants and snack kiosks inside Vinpearl Safari and nearby VinWonders. Afternoon: Explore VinWonders Phu Quoc – Vietnam’s Largest Theme Park After finishing at the Safari, take a short 5-minute ride by your private car or shuttle bus to VinWonders, another major entertainment hub next door. VinWonders Highlights: Six Themed Zones: European Avenue – Step into a fairytale world with castles, performances, and shopping. Fantasy World – Inspired by stories and legends from around the world. Ideal for kids. Typhoon World – A tropical water park with slides, a wave pool, and lazy rivers. Adventure World – Thrill-seekers can enjoy roller coasters, Viking rides, and 5D movies. Secret Village – Home to dwarves, elves, and magical beings. Kingdom of Ocean – An impressive aquarium with marine life shows and underwater tunnels. Live Shows & Entertainment: Magical parades and street performances are scheduled throughout the day. Kid Zones: Safe rides and games for toddlers and young children. Shaded rest areas, lockers, and food courts available throughout the park. Note: You can customize how much time you spend here. Guests usually spend about 3–4 hours. 🎭 19:00 – 19:30 | Free Walkthrough of Grand World’s Cultural & Art Zones Stroll and take photos in: European Square – Colorful architecture, fountains Bamboo Legend – Impressive bamboo art installation Vietnamese Urban Zone – Traditional-style buildings and lantern-lit streets Free entry 📸 Great for sunset and early evening photos 🧸 19:30 – 20:00 | Optional Visit – Teddy Bear Museum Explore whimsical bear-themed exhibits like Adventure Bears and Royal Bear Kingdom A favorite for kids, families, and social media lovers 🚪 Ticket (not included): ~150,000 VND/adult | ~100,000 VND/child 🕒 Visit time: ~30 minutes Optional – skip if not interested 🎭 20:00 – 20:45 | Optional: “The Quintessence of Vietnam” Grand Show 🌟 A grand-scale outdoor cultural performance with music, water, lights, and over 200 performers. 📍 Venue: Outdoor theater near Vietnamese Cultural Zone 🎟 Ticket (not included): Standard seating: ~300,000–400,000 VND/person Optional – guests may skip and continue exploring other parts of Grand World 🌉 20:45 – 21:30 | Venice Light Parade & Canal Show Stroll the Venetian-style canal under glowing lanterns Watch gondola dancers, music, and projected light shows over the water Grab a drink from a riverside café if desired Free to watch 💵 Optional drink: ~70,000–120,000 VND 🚌 21:30 – Return to Hotel Meet your private driver
